FAQ

Quick answers.

Can’t see yours? Ask us in store.

How long does a repair take?

Many repairs, like screens and batteries, are done the same day. We’ll give you an estimate when you bring your device in.

Do I need an appointment?

No — walk in whenever we’re open.

How much will it cost?

Diagnosis and quote are free. You’ll know the price before any work begins.

Do you use quality parts?

Yes. We use quality replacement parts for reliable, lasting repairs.

Is my data safe?

We treat your device and data with care. Backing up first is always a good idea.

Can you fix water damage?

Often, yes. Bring it in quickly and don’t try to charge it — we’ll assess it for free.

Which devices do you repair?

Smartphones, tablets, iPads, laptops and ultrabooks from all major brands.
