Questions.

How long does a repair take?

Many repairs, like screens and batteries, can be done the same day. We’ll give you an estimate once we’ve looked at your device.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Just walk in during opening hours and we’ll take a look.

How much will my repair cost?

We’ll diagnose the issue and give you a clear quote before starting the repair. You decide whether you’d like us to go ahead. Diagnosis is free.

What happens during diagnosis?

We check your device, find the fault and explain your options. You get a clear quote before we start.

Do you use quality parts?

Yes. We use quality replacement parts for reliable, lasting repairs.

Is my data safe?

We take care with your device and your data. Backing up before a repair is always a good idea.

Can you fix water damage?

Often, yes, but it depends on the damage. Bring it in as soon as you can and don’t try to charge it. We’ll assess it for free.

Which devices do you repair?

Smartphones, tablets, iPads, laptops and ultrabooks from all major brands.
