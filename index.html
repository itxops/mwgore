FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a repair take?

Many repairs, such as screens and batteries, can be done the same day. We'll give you an estimate when you bring your device in.

Do I need an appointment?

No — you're welcome to walk in during opening hours.

How much will it cost?

Diagnostics and quotes are free. We'll tell you the price before any work begins.

Do you use quality parts?

Yes, we use quality replacement parts for reliable, lasting repairs.

Is my data safe?

We treat your device and data with care. We still recommend backing up before any repair where possible.

Can you fix water-damaged devices?

Often, yes. Bring it in as soon as possible and don't try to charge it — we'll assess it for free.

Which devices do you repair?

Smartphones, tablets, iPads, laptops and ultrabooks from all major brands.
